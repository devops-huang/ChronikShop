## 1. 风险（按"会不会把项目做死"排序）· Risks, ordered by "can this kill the project"

| # | 风险 · Risk | 影响 · Impact | 应对 · Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **R1** | **HIPAA**：医疗采购单上可能出现患者信息（居家护理客户的单子尤其）<br>Patient data may appear on POs, especially home-health customers | 合规事故 / compliance incident | 默认**不落盘患者数据**：schema 不含患者字段、日志不落文档文本；上线前请客户 compliance 确认 BAA；Azure OpenAI 需在支持 BAA 的区域/配置下使用；**M1 前澄清**<br>No patient fields in the schema, no document text in logs; BAA and region confirmed before M1 |
| **R2** | **料号 crosswalk 不全**（客户料号 ↔ legacy ↔ ERP SKU 只有 Excel 且不干净）<br>Crosswalk is incomplete or messy | 匹配率上不去，全部落人工 / everything falls to humans | M1 第 1 天做覆盖率体检，输出"多少行只能靠描述匹配"；覆盖率 <80% 则加"主数据清洗"另行计费<br>Audit coverage on day one and report it; below ~80% we scope a data-cleanup add-on |
| **R3** | **"未见过的格式"与验收准确率矛盾**<br>"Unseen formats" vs an accuracy promise | 承诺做不到的自动率 / over-promise | 分层验收（干净 / 扫描 / 手写）+ hold-out 格式测试（掉点 ≤5）<br>Tiered acceptance plus a hold-out format test |
| **R4** | **复核体验被低估**：flag 太多太杂 → 代表全部忽略 → 项目失败<br>Flag fatigue kills adoption | **项目失败，不是延期** / project failure | 复核页必须"能指到字"；三级 flag；M1 就交给真实代表计时；设"每日 flag 密度"指标（≤2.5 条/单）<br>Crops, three severities, time-tested with real reps in M1, flag-density target ≤2.5 per order |
| **R5** | **CSV 规范由第三方定、可能反复改**<br>CSV spec owned by a third party | 返工、扯皮 / rework | 声明式列规范，改格式 = 改配置 + 回归；含 2 次修订，超出按 $135/h<br>Declarative spec; two revisions included |
| **R6** | **邮件侧权限**（共享邮箱、Front、传真落在哪个邮箱）<br>Mail permissions and the fax mailbox | 卡在 IT，第一周无法开工 / blocked week one | 第 1 天交权限清单；备选"目录监听模式"先跑起来<br>Permission checklist on day one; folder-watch mode as fallback |
| **R7** | **模型/API 变更**（版本退役、价格变动）<br>Model or API changes | 上线后失效 / post-launch breakage | prompt/模型名/成本表都在配置里；回归集在 CI 跑，换模型先过测试再切<br>Config-driven; model swaps must pass the regression suite |
| **R8** | 订单量远超假设（如 2000 单/日）<br>Volume far above assumption | 成本与吞吐 / cost and throughput | 成本随单量线性（$0.02–0.05/单），吞吐靠横向扩；SOW 写明量级假设<br>Linear cost, horizontal scale, volume written into the SOW |
| **R9** | 客户内部"我们用 Copilot 也能做"的声音<br>"We could do this with Copilot" | 丢单 / lost deal | 给 A/B 对比数据：PA/Copilot 原生方案在旋转、多页、证据回查、回归测试上的缺失<br>Show measured route-A vs route-B accuracy on their own samples |
| **R10** | 单价/合同价差异频繁<br>Frequent price differences | 复核量上升，客户抱怨"总报错" / noise | `PRICE_MISMATCH` 默认 REVIEW 而非 BLOCK；价格策略可配置；月度报表指出哪家客户价格最不一致<br>Review (not block) by default, configurable price policy, monthly report per customer |

## 2. 首次会议必问（按优先级）· First-call questions, by priority

**数据与范围 · Data & scope**

1. 订单量：日均/峰值？未来 12 个月增长？<br>*Daily and peak volume? 12-month growth?*
2. 客户数量与格式分布：Top 10 占多少？多少客户一年只来几单？（长尾决定"未见格式"的真实压力）<br>*Customer count and format spread; how much is Top 10? The long tail drives the unseen-format load.*
3. 文档构成：系统导出 / 扫描 / 传真 / 手写 各占多少？（决定 M2 是 4 周还是 6 周）<br>*Mix of born-digital / scan / fax / handwriting — this alone decides 4 weeks vs 6.*
4. 样本单能覆盖"最脏"的那 10 份吗？（比 100 份干净样本有用得多）<br>*Can you include the ten ugliest documents? They beat a hundred clean ones.*

**主数据 · Master data**

5. 料号体系：ERP SKU / 客户料号 / legacy / UPC 的对应关系在哪？谁维护？多久更新？<br>*Where does the crosswalk live, who maintains it, how often?*
6. 客户料号是否跨客户复用？（有的话匹配规则要变）<br>*Are customer part numbers ever reused across customers?*
7. 价目表：每客户一份合同价？价格组？有没有促销价/临时价？<br>*Contract price per customer, price groups, promotional prices?*
8. 收货地址：一个客户多少地址？有"码头/科室"这类非结构化信息吗？<br>*How many addresses per customer? Dock/ward text in them?*

**流程与系统 · Process & systems**

9. CSV 规范谁定？集成商联系人？M1 前能否给一版草案？<br>*Who owns the CSV spec; can we get a draft before M1?*
10. ERP 投递目录是 SMB / SFTP / Blob？写权限怎么给？<br>*How is the drop folder exposed and who grants write access?*
11. 复核环节：代表一天多少单？每单多久？用什么工具？<br>*Reps: volume, minutes per order, tooling.*
12. 传真落在哪个邮箱？M365 传真连接器还是外部服务？<br>*Which mailbox receives faxes, and via what?*
13. 重复单：集成商已查重，但同一 PO 重发我们是否仍出 CSV？（建议：出，文件名 attempt 递增并标记）<br>*If the same PO arrives twice, do we still emit? (Recommended: yes, with an incremented attempt.)*

**合规与运维 · Compliance & operations**

14. 会遇到含患者信息的文档吗？需要 BAA 吗？Azure 区域有要求吗？<br>*Patient data? BAA needed? Region constraints?*
15. 谁拥有 Azure 订阅？谁能建资源？IT 变更审批多久？（经常是真正的关键路径）<br>*Who owns the subscription and approves changes? This is often the real critical path.*
16. 出问题找谁？需要 24/7 吗？（不需要就按工作日报价）<br>*Escalation path and support hours?*
17. 成功标准：6 个月后你们希望看到什么数字？（把这句话变成验收指标）<br>*What numbers do you want to see in six months? Turn that sentence into the acceptance metric.*

## 3. 我们主动放弃的东西（建立信任）· What we explicitly say we will NOT do

- ❌ 不做按客户模板的训练/配置（客户已否；也确实维护不动）<br>*No per-customer templates — the client rejected them and they cannot be maintained.*
- ❌ 不承诺"全自动放行"——目标是**让人需要看的单子少而准**<br>*No promise of full automation — the goal is a small, accurate review queue.*
- ❌ 不接管 ERP 导入、查重、审批（集成商负责）<br>*No ERP import, duplicate check or approval workflow.*
- ❌ 不做发票/装箱单/ASN（可另立里程碑）<br>*No invoices, packing lists or ASNs (separate milestone).*
- ❌ 不按单收费、不转售 AI 用量、不做 SaaS 托管<br>*No per-order fees, no reselling tokens, no SaaS hosting.*

## 4. 提案钩子（可直接用）· Proposal hook

> **EN** — The hard part of this project is not reading PDFs — it is proving that what was read is right. Most vendors will show you an extraction demo; we will show you a **flag with a cropped screenshot of the exact line on the page**, an arithmetic reconciliation that catches a missing page, and a regression suite that tells you the truth about accuracy on *your* worst documents. That is what makes a rep trust the queue.
> **中文** — 这个项目难的不是"把 PDF 读出来"，而是**证明读出来的东西是对的**。别人给你看抽取 demo，我们给你看三样东西：**每条疑点都附上原文那一行的截图**、能抓出漏页的算术对账、以及在**你自己最脏的文档**上讲真话的回归测试。这才是一个复核员敢信这个队列的原因。
